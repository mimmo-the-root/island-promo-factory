Default skins (your own copy, never published: Epic Games artwork).

Download the official Fortnite skin renders from the Epic Games brand portal:
https://brand.epicgames.com/document/469

Put transparent PNG renders of Fortnite outfits here (any size, one full-body figure each; the name in brackets,
for example "FN 01 Outfit (Skull Trooper).png", becomes the character description).
A map with NO character of its own gets one of them at random (scripts/default_skin.py); the pick is saved in the map and kept.
A skin you put in the map is always used instead. Another one: python scripts\default_skin.py --project <map> --reroll
