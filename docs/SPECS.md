# Creator Portal media specs (checked 15 Oct 2026, mandatory from 22 Oct 2026)

Source of truth: `scripts/epic_specs.json`. Always verify on the Fortnite Creator Portal before uploading.

| Asset | Spec |
|---|---|
| Landscape thumbnail | 1920x1080 PNG/JPEG, <= 5 MB. Two versions: art + logo, and art only |
| Portrait thumbnail | 1440x1920 (3:4), <= 5 MB. Two versions: art + logo, and art only |
| Logo | 1440x608 transparent PNG |
| Lobby background | 2048x1024 PNG/JPG, <= 10 MB, optional |
| Video | 15-30 s, MOV ProRes, 16:9, native fps, uncompressed stereo audio (kit renders 1920x1080) |
| In-game screenshots | count as island metadata: real gameplay only |

Thumbnails may contain only the logo as text. Keep a 200 px top/bottom safe area and the bottom corners free (badges).
Forbidden: blood, guns aimed at the viewer/head, crosshair, V-Bucks/coins, XP/AFK/level-up, platform buttons, assets not in the island,
Epic logos/key art, real people, copies of other islands.
Gameplay footage must be real and unedited, including the UI and the pre-game lobby.
