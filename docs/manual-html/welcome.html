Welcome

Island Promo Factory builds everything the Fortnite Creator Portal asks for your UEFN island, on your own PC, one map at a time: thumbnails, logo, lobby background, gameplay video, trailer and screenshots.

You give it three things: a screenshot of your island's environment, a character render and a gameplay recording. It gives you the numbered files for the portal fields, at the right size, inside the safe areas, validated against Epic's current rules.

How it works

What it does for you

  • Makes the landscape and portrait thumbnails (with the logo, and art only), the 1440x608 logo and the lobby background.
  • Cuts your real gameplay recording into the portal video and builds a trailer with your real clips, your island code and the age-rating badge.
  • Checks every file against the rules in Epic rules and tells you what to fix.
  • Optionally makes vertical videos for social media with English promotional subtitles (Social videos).

What it does not do

Warning

It never uploads anything. You upload the files yourself, and you are responsible for following Epic's rules. Epic changes them: check the portal.

  • It does not include Fortnite skins or art. You supply your own, or download Epic's official renders yourself (First map).
  • It is an unofficial tool, not affiliated with Epic Games.

Two ways to use it

With Claude Code you open the folder and type /promo-pack: Claude asks for what it needs and runs every step. Without Claude everything also runs from plain commands (new_map.bat, run_all.bat, dashboard.bat). Start with the Quick start.
