What you get, slot by slot

Everything for the portal is in Projects\my-map\promo_pack\, numbered like the portal fields. final\ only holds working files.

FilePortal slot
01_landscape_with_text and 02_landscape_art_onlyLandscape thumbnail, with logo and art only
03_portrait_with_text and 04_portrait_art_onlyPortrait thumbnail, with logo and art only
05_logoIsland logo, transparent PNG
06_lobby_backgroundLobby background (optional)
07_gameplayGameplay video: one continuous, unedited cut of your recording with the original audio
08_trailerTrailer built from your real clips, with the end card and badges
09_screenshot_1..3In-game screenshots
UPLOAD_CHECKLIST.mdWhat to tick before you upload

Videos come twice: an MP4 and a ProRes MOV (_prores.mov). Epic asks for MOV ProRes, so upload that one.

Note

The gameplay slot is deliberately unedited: Epic wants real gameplay with the game UI. The polished montage, if you ask for it, lives in promo_pack\social\ and is not for the portal.

lightbox.png puts everything on one board so you can check it in a glance.
