Epic rules

These are the rules the kit checks, taken from scripts/epic_specs.json, the single source used by the validator, the promo pack and this page.

AssetRule
Landscape thumbnail1920x1080 PNG/JPEG, up to 5 MB; two versions: art + logo, art only
Portrait thumbnail1440x1920 PNG/JPEG, up to 5 MB; two versions: art + logo, art only
Logo1440x608 transparent PNG
Lobby background2048x1024 PNG/JPG, up to 10 MB (optional)
Video15-30 s, MOV ProRes, 16:9, native frame rate, uncompressed stereo audio (the kit renders 1920x1080)
Safe areakeep titles 200 px away from the top and bottom edges

Verified on 2026-10-15; mandatory from 2026-10-22.

What may not appear on a thumbnail

  • Text other than the logo. Age ratings and "Developed in Fortnite" belong in videos only.
  • Blood, weapons pointed at the viewer or at a head, crosshairs.
  • V-Bucks or coins, XP, AFK or level-up messages, platform buttons.
  • Assets that are not in your island, Epic logos or key art, real people, copies of other islands.

The negative prompts of the AI artwork and the upload checklist cover these, but looking is still your job: UPLOAD_CHECKLIST.md has a checkbox for each.

Videos

Gameplay must be real and unedited, with the game UI and the pre-game lobby. The trailer may be edited. Music in a Discover request must come from the Licensed Music Box (Music).

Warning

Epic updates these rules. The kit shows the date it last verified them; check the Creator Portal before you upload.
