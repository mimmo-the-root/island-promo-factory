FAQ

Does it upload to the portal for me?

No. You upload the files yourself.

Can I use it without a GPU?

Yes: the Light setup. You bring the landscape art and the music is synthesised.

Why is my gameplay video not edited?

Epic asks for real, unedited gameplay with the game UI. The kit cuts one continuous piece of your recording. An edited montage is available for social media only.

Is the Fortnite art included?

No. Supply your own skins, or download Epic's official renders yourself.

Can it make videos for TikTok?

Yes, as an optional step: see Social videos.

How do I make a second map?

Create another map folder. The kit works on one map at a time, and the similarity check warns if two maps look alike.

Where are my results?

Projects\<map>\promo_pack\.
