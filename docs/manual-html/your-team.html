Your team (agents and models)

With Claude Code the kit comes with seven small agents in .claude/agents/. Cheap models do the mechanical work, a stronger one judges what needs eyes. None uses Opus.

AgentModelJob
promo-intakeHaikuRuns the intake and reports BLOCK and WARN lines
promo-runnerHaikuStarts a run and reads only the status and the log tail
epic-complianceHaikuMechanical checks from epic_specs.json
social-copywriterHaikuDrafts the English subtitles
release-managerHaikuVersion, changelog and test chores; never publishes
art-directorSonnetJudges the thumbnails from one contact sheet
kit-auditorSonnetOn request: audits the kit

The rules that keep token use low are in CLAUDE.md: short replies, no polling, no whole-file reads of big logs or images. python scripts\agent_cost.py (or the console's /costs) shows where tokens went.
